The Royal Army Medical Corps

The RAMC has a proud tradition of providing medical care to the British Army in peace and conflict.

From the battlefields of the past to modern military operations, RAMC personnel have remained dedicated to the care of those who serve.

About Us

The RAMC Association Ulster Branch brings together past and serving members of the Royal Army Medical Corps and those who served alongside them.

We are united by shared experiences, mutual respect and an enduring commitment to the RAMC community.
