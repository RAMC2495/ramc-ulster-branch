About the RAMC

The Royal Army Medical Corps has served alongside the British Army for generations, providing medical care, support and expertise wherever it has been required.

Our Heritage

The RAMC has a proud history of caring for soldiers in conflict, during operations and in peacetime. From field ambulances and medical units to modern battlefield medicine, its personnel have consistently placed the care of others at the centre of their service.

Our Community

The RAMC community extends beyond those who wore the RAMC cap badge. It includes the people, units and supporting arms who served alongside us and contributed to the medical mission.

About Us

The Royal Army Medical Corps Association – Ulster Branch exists to support our members, preserve our shared history and maintain the strong bonds created through service.

We welcome former members of the RAMC and those from its supporting arms who served alongside us. Our door is always open to those who shared our service, regardless of which cap badge they wore.

We recognise that the RAMC could not have fulfilled its role without the support of the wider military community. Just as we supported those we were providing medical care for, we will always value and support those who served alongside us.

Our branch is about friendship, remembrance, welfare, community and ensuring that the traditions and history of the RAMC continue to be valued by future generations.
